An AI agent is a language model in a loop that uses tools to affect the world.

The model thinks. A controller behind it, the harness, keeps the loop running, calls tools, and feeds the results back to the model. Round after round, until the task is done.

01Think. The model plans the next step.
02Act. The harness calls a tool.
03Observe. The result flows back.

Think, act, observe.

The loop runs until the task is done, sometimes two rounds, sometimes two hundred.

The fundamental choice for any project. Toggle to see both sides.

Runnable locally or at a host of your choice, with full control over your data. The price: your own hardware and some setup.

The strongest performance with no effort, but your data flows through the vendor.

In 2026, open models reach near-frontier quality for the first time. The choice: control & privacy, or convenience & peak performance with no effort.

Open weights: host or run locally?

You can run an open model yourself: on your own machine, or on a rented one. Renting means you pay for what you use and need no hardware. Running it yourself means you need enough memory — how much depends on the size of the model. The number before the B counts its parameters in billions: 7B is small, 70B is large.
